# Micro Rex first, Nano Rex next

## 1. Micro Rex — same joints

Keep Microduck's 14 policy joints, XL330 actuator class, joint geometry, sensors and 61→14 policy interface. Improve the dinosaur shell, retention, maintenance and presentation. Re-run the unchanged official checkpoint whenever added geometry changes mass, inertia or contacts.

Current Rev C changes: continuous rounded cheeks, larger visible eye whites, M2 rear eye retention, strap slots in both head bridges, rounded forearm corners, and a README portrait rendered from the actual CAD.

Physical acceptance remains open: eye pull/torque tests, shell retention under vibration, cable and camera clearances, repeated head/joint motion, tail mounting, drop testing, thermal tests and actual gait trials. Do not describe an untested shell as tougher than Microduck. Start with coupons and one assembled head before ordering a batch.

## 2. Nano Rex — lower cost

Deferred until the same-joint Micro Rex is validated. Compare actuator, battery, electronics and assembly costs using supplier quotations and loaded-joint measurements. Price and BOM are not fixed yet.

Changing actuator torque, link lengths, joint count or mass distribution invalidates any assumption that the Microduck checkpoint will transfer unchanged. Nano Rex must have its own transfer evaluation and may require retraining. No Nano Rex manufacturing files or claimed price are released yet.

Q4 is retired from the public Micro X viewer; its previous CAD is retained as historical work.

The source hardware's noncommercial terms remain separate from the Apache-2.0 policy weights. Commercial hardware rights need to be resolved before selling a derivative robot.
